## Reusable scoring rubric

Rate each criterion **0–10**; contribution = **weight × rating / 10**. Weights total **10**. A 7 is competent but incomplete. Score deployed evidence, not unpublished potential; mark untested areas provisional. Fabricated claims invalidate a 10.

| Criterion | Weight | A 10 looks like | A 7 looks like |
|---|---:|---|---|
| First-five-second clarity | 1.5 | Name, role, Lagos, distinctive value and strongest proof immediately legible. | Role/location clear; generic headline or proof below fold. |
| Verifiable evidence density | 2.0 | Major claims have ownership, scoped outcomes and accessible evidence or clearly stated confidentiality limits. | Credible numbers and links, but attribution or verification requires inference. |
| Case-study depth | 1.5 | Selected work explains problem, constraints, decisions, tradeoffs, personal contribution, validation and outcome. | Useful summaries explain features/stack, with limited decisions or validation. |
| AI-skill proof | 1.5 | Evaluation method, clearly separated review/approval outcomes, public demonstration and shipped AI product. | Relevant role and methods described; results or inspectable demonstration incomplete. |
| Visual craft and usability | 1.5 | Distinctive, coherent typography/layout; effortless navigation; no collisions across screen sizes. | Polished and readable with minor spacing, density or interaction weaknesses. |
| Performance, a11y and SEO | 1.0 | Measured performance, keyboard/reduced-motion checks, accessible semantics and crawlable content/metadata. | Basic semantics, metadata and responsiveness; some verification or rendering gaps. |
| Hiring/contact conversion | 1.0 | Persistent contact, concise résumé, relevant profiles and direct routes from work to conversation. | Email/socials easy to find; résumé or decision context missing. |
